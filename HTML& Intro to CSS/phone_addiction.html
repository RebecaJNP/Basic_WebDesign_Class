Addiction? What Addiction? It's an addiction

"Everything is on my phone"

Its a big problem, tied to other issues in our lives

This addiction is so new that there is not official diagnoses therefore there are limited ways to treat it. However, there have been more awareness to this and there are now some rehabs for phone addiction, online and sometimes in person. The phone addiction had ties with neglecting other things in your life, like school, work, and personal relationships. For many people who are in school our phones have become something we focuses more of our time on then actually doing school work. With the rise of other technology advances in AI being more accusable for people to use however they like. This things make us neglect things like our education or over health, either physical and/or mental.

How can I be free from phone addiction?

As our phone become an addiction and as we realize it one of most normal conclusion is that we need to stop using phones, only use them for calls and other things. However if you are used to using your phone for hours just stopping all of a sudden will not work, you'll end up just spending hours on it after a day or so. The way your phone keeps you addicted is by making your brain release chemicals to the brain making you feel euphoric keeping you addicted. Slowly doing things to reduce the time you spend on your phone, like picking up hobbies that bring you joy. Or setting a certain amount of time away from your phone and slowly build up over time. Leave your phone somewhere out of sight when doing something important. There are several ways to be able to start but it's important that we start slow. And this doesn't mean that we have to stop using phones all together but just stop relying on it as the center of our day.

Things to try instead of using your phone:

  • Go out for a walk
  • Hang out with love ones
  • Invest in a hobby you find interesting
  • Journaling and/or meditation
